## Changes required to the earlier proposal

The existing separate Allottee -> Allotment -> Shop relationships remain appropriate, with the one-shop case prefilled visibly. The previous proposal is incomplete for these notices in five important respects:

1. A fixed monthly rent field alone loses actual area, accepted rate and annual revision history.
2. An end date alone cannot represent physical occupancy, vacation or deposit-refund review.
3. A generic renewal link alone does not enforce the original award's extension limit or record approval/performance/fee evidence.
4. Preserving the existing five-day receipt rule contradicts the user's direction to use the notices' terms. Financial changes need reviewed examples and a migration policy; this document does not recalculate saved records.
5. Bills/payments alone do not record held security, refunds, one-off extension fees or incident fines correctly. These obligations require distinct accounting treatment and export/audit coverage.

The revised design must include these records/workflows in staff screens, Admin, search, dashboard attention, dues reports, data transfer and correction history. It must not claim whole-estate readiness while excluding them. New calculations need Decimal arithmetic and focused tests, including date boundaries, April increases, extension limits, deposit rounding, partial payments, dispatch evidence and outstanding-debt visibility after handover.

## Remaining calculation decisions

The [staff workflow](ESTATE-WORKFLOW.md) explains the implemented rules. The notice review below preserves the source interpretation.

The notices specify the governing conditions but do not provide executable examples for every case. Before implementing the financial policy, settle:

- When invoice raising and email transmission occur on different dates, how do clauses 7.6 and 7.15 combine for the seven-day deadline and interest commencement? Record both dates rather than overwriting one with the other.
- Confirm the annual basis/day divisor and charge components for the 12% simple-interest calculation, partial-payment allocation and rounding. The sentence specifies daily calculation but does not itself spell out every algorithmic detail.
- Confirm annual-increase rounding, late-award treatment, and whether a rate already entered includes past April increases. Never apply an increase twice.
- Confirm the exact-hundred boundary for deposit rounding, approved security deductions and fee/fine tax treatment before posting those amounts.
- Agree whether existing saved bills need reviewed corrections under this policy, or whether their recorded financial values remain as the legacy history. Never infer replacement amounts from advertised areas or unfilled bid forms.

These are specific accounting/office decisions, not a reason to omit the shop, term, occupancy, source-reference and evidence records from the design.
